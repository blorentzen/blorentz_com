## 2. Services

### 2.1 Nature of the Website and Marketing Audit

The Website and Marketing Audit is a fixed-scope consulting engagement that provides:

- A live recorded review session of up to 60 minutes, conducted via Google Meet
- A written assessment typically delivered within 5–7 days of the session, depending on bandwidth
- A 30-minute walkthrough call after the written assessment is delivered (with flexibility to extend if useful)
- Email follow-up for clarifying questions on the audit content
- Access to a client portal hosted at blorentz.com/audits/[client-slug] for twelve (12) months from the date of audit delivery
- A downloadable archive containing the written report (PDF) and session recording (MP4)

The Roadmap tier additionally includes specific app, service, and tool recommendations for self-implementation, prioritized by commercial impact, and a more detailed walkthrough.

The Works tier additionally includes implementation of audit findings as a custom-scoped engagement, with the audit fee credited 100% toward the engagement when booked within ninety (90) days of audit delivery.

- Empac and Britton Lorentzen are not security professionals and make no representations or warranties about the security posture of any audited product.

The Client is solely responsible for engaging qualified security professionals to perform formal security assessments. Audit recommendations do not substitute for such assessments.

### 2.3 Implementation

The Audit and Roadmap tiers are diagnostic engagements. They do not include implementation work.

The Works tier includes implementation of audit findings as a custom-scoped engagement, with scope and pricing determined based on the audit findings and locked in writing before any implementation work begins. The Works engagement starts at $10,000 USD; final pricing depends on scope.

If you book an Audit or Roadmap and subsequently book The Works within ninety (90) days of audit delivery, the audit fee will be credited 100% toward the engagement. Credit applies only to The Works engagements; it does not apply to Optimization Plans or other recurring services.
